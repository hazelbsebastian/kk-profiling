What is KK Profiling?

Every youth aged 15 to 30 living in Barangay Bantug is part of the Katipunan ng Kabataan.

Your profile helps the SK know how many youth live in each purok, so programs, scholarships and events reach the right people.

What you'll need:

  • Your full name
  • Your birthday
  • Your purok
  • About 3 minutes

Who can
register?

In-school youth

Students in high school, senior high, college or vocational school.

Out of school youth

Youth not currently enrolled in any school.

Working youth

Youth who are employed or running their own business.

Unemployed

Youth who are looking for work or not working right now.
